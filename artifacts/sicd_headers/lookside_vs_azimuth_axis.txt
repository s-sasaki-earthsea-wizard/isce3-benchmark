Azimuth-axis direction vs look side in SICD RGZERO/INCA products
================================================================

Question: is the reversed azimuth axis on the Capella Mexico City pair a
Capella quirk, or a consequence of the SICD standard?

Answer: the standard. NGA's own reference implementation derives Grid/Col from
the geometry for an INCA grid (ngageoint/MATLAB_SAR,
IO/complex/sicd/derived_sicd_fields.m, INCA branch):

    uRG  = (SCP - ca_pos) / |SCP - ca_pos|          % range unit vector
    left = cross(ca_pos/|ca_pos|, ca_vel/|ca_vel|)
    look = sign(left . uRG)                          % +1 = Left, -1 = Right
    spn  = -look * cross(uRG, ca_vel);  spn /= |spn| % slant plane normal
    uAZ  = cross(spn, uRG)
    Grid.Row.UVectECF = uRG
    Grid.Col.UVectECF = uAZ

Because uRG is perpendicular to ca_vel at closest approach,
(uRG x v_hat) x uRG = v_hat exactly, so this reduces to

    Grid/Col/UVectECF = -look * v_hat

i.e. the azimuth axis is ANTI-parallel to the velocity for a left-looking
collect and parallel for a right-looking one. Zero-Doppler time therefore
DECREASES with column index for every left-looking RGZERO product, whoever
the producer is. The same expression appears in the RMAT branch of the same
file as `uYAT = -look*VelRef/norm(VelRef)`.

Both Mexico City scenes (2024-06-26, 2024-06-29) are side L, TimeCAPoly[1] < 0,
uCol.v/|v| = -1.000000.

sign(TimeCAPoly[1]) == sign(uCol . v) == -look in all 8 cases, with uCol . v_hat
equal to +/-1 to 6 decimal places, so uCol is exactly (anti)parallel to the
velocity as the derivation requires.

Consequence for the converter: do NOT hard-code the azimuth flip. Branch on
sign(TimeCAPoly[1]), and flip the image, the validSamples rows and the
start/end times together. A right-looking RGZERO SICD needs no flip at all.
